# LITERATURE — Vortex density in random wave fields

Honesty rule: "no matching study found in sources searched" — never "novel".

## Core references

- J. F. Nye & M. V. Berry (1974), "Dislocations in wave trains",
  Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 336, 165-190. Foundational: phase singularities ("wave
  dislocations") in 1-D and 2-D wave fields; density scaling.
- M. V. Berry (1978), "Dislocations in wave trains and the propagation of
  dislocations", in *The Physics of Defects* (Les Houches). Propagation of vortices.
- M. V. Berry (2000), "Phase singularities in isotropic random waves",
  Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 456, 2059-2079. Isotropic random-wave dislocation density.
- M. V. Berry & M. R. Dennis (2001), "Polarization singularities in isotropic random
  vector waves", ibid. 457, 141-155. (Vector extension; not used here.)
- I. Freund (1998), "Dynamics of optical vortices in the microwave speckle field",
  and related speckle-vortex studies. Vortex dynamics and density in speckle.
- M. R. Dennis (2001), "Local phase structure of wave dislocation lines".
- Kac-Rice formula: S. O. Rice (1944-45), "Mathematical analysis of random noise";
  M. Kac (1943). Standard tool for zero/level-crossing densities; used for the
  analytic prediction.

## Textbook / tertiary

- Goodman, *Speckle Phenomena in Optics* (2007): speckle statistics and vortex
  ("dark") density in fully developed speckle.
- Berry (1981), "Singularities in waves and rays".

## What the sources state relevant here

- Isotropic random waves host phase singularities of signed unit charge, in
  charge-neutral pairs.
- The mean dislocation density follows from the second moment of the field and its
  derivatives (Kac-Rice), and for an isotropic narrow-band field scales as k0^2.
