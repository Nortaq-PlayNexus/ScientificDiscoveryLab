# INFORMATION THEORY — research map

## Known
- Kolmogorov-complexity surrogates (LZ/gzip) are widely used and widely abused;
  statistical calibration is the live issue.
- Transfer entropy vs Granger equivalence/false-positive behaviour documented.
- PRNG testing standards exist (NIST SP 800-22, Diehard).

## Computable
- Compression-based screens, causality-estimator calibration, PRNG batteries: all
  cheap and HIGHLY relevant to lab hygiene (I001, I002, I003, I004).

## Guiding principle
- The lab only trusts "suspicious pattern" screens that have been power/calibration
  tested on known ground truth first (I003/I004) — otherwise they produce noise
  dressed in confidence.
