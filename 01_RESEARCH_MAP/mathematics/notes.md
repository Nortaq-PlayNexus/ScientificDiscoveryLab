# MATHEMATICS — research map

## Known
- Collatz (3n+1): unproven; deep heuristics; extensive empirical data.
- Prime gaps: Gallagher Poisson model; Hardy-Littlewood conditioning; modern
  statistics literature.
- Normalcy of pi/e unproven; huge digit-scan data pass standard tests.
- Feigenbaum constants: delta(2)=4.669..., order-conjugation universal structure.
- Agreements: exact critical exponents in 1D sandpile; Abel sandpile 2D numerics well
  acted on.
- Dishonest statistical traps: multiple testing over scans (digit/prime surveys).

## Open / computable
- Systematic, FDR-clean statistical surveys (M001-M006) — mostly reproduction and
  hygiene projects with honest error bars; genuine novelty is unlikely but the
  method value is real (a controlled "scan" tool for the lab).
