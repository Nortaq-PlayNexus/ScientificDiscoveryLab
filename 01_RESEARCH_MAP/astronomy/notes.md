# ASTRONOMY / COSMOLOGY — research map

## Known
- Transit method standard; public catalogs exist. GAIA photometry calibrated.
  CMB large-angle anomalies reported and heavily debated; masks/systematics dominate
  interpretations.
- Fireball/meteor climatology known; observability biases dominate clustering.

## Computable on this machine
- Light curve folding, BLS grids: yes. HEALPix: NOT installed (barrier for CMB).
- Big public downloads are the main practical hurdle; all A candidates are MEDIUM or
  LOW feasibility until data is curated and cached locally.

## Honest role
- Reproduction of known detections/calibrations with quantified recoverability; any
  "candidate" is automatically suspect (catalog contamination) until external vetting.
