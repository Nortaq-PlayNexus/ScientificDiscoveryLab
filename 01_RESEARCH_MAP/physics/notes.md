# PHYSICS — research map

## Known
- Statistical optics (see optics map). Random-matrix universality (GOE/GUE), graph
  spectral statistics active. FPUT recurrences established (soliton connection).
  Percolation exact thresholds for common lattices. Sandpile exponents.
- Classical "quantum-looking" correlations are bounded by Cauchy-Schwarz / Glauber
  inequalities — classical fields cannot violate them.

## Open / ambit
- The lab's honest role: reproduction of published scaling laws with tight error bars,
  and a falsification discipline capable of detecting numerical mimicry of "physics".
- No experiment here has the means to establish new physics; it can only produce
  candidates for external human review.

## Computable
- 1D/2D classical field sims (sandbox), ODE/chain integrators, spectral statistics
  (scipy EVD to ~4000x4000), percolation/piling on grids to 1024^2.
