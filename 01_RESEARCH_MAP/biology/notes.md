# BIOLOGY — research map

## Known
- Gillespie SSA exact for reaction kinetics; bistable positive-feedback circuits
  bimodal in deterministic parameter regimes, smeared by finite populations.
- GC-skew/CpG methods standard; autocorrelation inflates naive significance.

## Computable
- SSA simulations: cheap. Public small genomes: cheap downloads. The honest projects
  (B001, B002) quantify methodological reliability, not biological discovery.

## Guardrail
- No medical claims may be made from any lab computation. Public computational
  datasets only. See RESEARCH_RULES.md.
