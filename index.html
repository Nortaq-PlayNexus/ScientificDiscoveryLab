What this is

A research harness for running falsifiable investigations and recording what each one is actually allowed to claim. Every result is placed on an explicit evidence ladder — a hypothesis does not become a finding by being interesting.

Evidence ladder

Highest support a conclusion may claim here:

  • UNTESTED
  • INITIAL RESULT
  • CONTROLLED
  • REPLICATED
  • INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED
  • LITERATURE-CHECKED
  • EXTERNALLY VALIDATED

Repository

  • Source — harness, investigations and test suite
  • EXPERIMENT_REGISTRY.md — every experiment and its current state
  • DISCOVERY_LOG.md — chronological record
  • RESEARCH_RULES.md — the rules results are held to
  • HANDOFF.md — current state of work
